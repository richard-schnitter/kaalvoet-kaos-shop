How ordering works

There is no card checkout here. You reserve what you want, pay by EFT, and send me the proof. Simple and fee-free.

1Pick your gear

Add discs and kit to your bag. Discs are one-of-one, so a disc in someone else's bag is still available until they actually pay — first proof of payment wins.

2Send your details

Name, cell, the team you play for and where you are based. That is how I know which tournament to bring it to, or where to courier it.

3EFT and upload the POP

You get an order number, and the banking details to pay into. EFT the total using that number as the reference, then send me a screenshot of the payment. No card, no fees.

4I confirm and hand over

You get a WhatsApp from me confirming stock and arranging the hand-over — training, a tournament, collection or courier.
