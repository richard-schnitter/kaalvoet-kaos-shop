The crate

Every disc is photographed individually. What you see is the exact disc you get — no two are the same, and once one is gone it is gone.

How ordering works

There is no card checkout here. You reserve what you want, pay by EFT, and send me the proof. Simple and fee-free.

1Pick your gear

Add whatever you like to your bag — pick as many as you want. Every disc is one-of-one, so if a few people ask for the same one I will come back to you and sort out who gets what before anyone pays.

2Send your details

Name, cell, the team you play for and where you are based. That is how I know which tournament to bring it to, or who can carry it your way. South African orders only.

3EFT and upload the POP

Once I have confirmed which discs are yours, I send the banking details and your order number. EFT using that number as the reference, then send me a screenshot. No card, no fees.

4I confirm and hand over

You get a WhatsApp from me confirming stock and arranging the hand-over — training, a tournament, collection, or sent down with someone heading your way.
